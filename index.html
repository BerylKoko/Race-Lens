03 / DESIGN & EVALUATION

What are we testing?

A map-first historical replay that helps first-time viewers answer questions without leaving the screen to look up terminology.

INFO 4900
Prof. Huang review
DESIGN RATIONALE

Progressive disclosure

The map, running order and tyres establish context; Driver Focus reveals one driver; Compare reveals a second only when requested. Qualifying and telemetry live on a separate level to avoid crowding the race view.

Question-mark explanations appear where terminology is used, rather than making novice viewers consult a detached glossary.

DATA PROVENANCE

What is real—and what isn't?

  • Verified: qualifying Q1/Q2/Q3 times, selected on-track position snapshots, pit-stop laps, amended final standings, selected gaps and best race sectors.
  • Teaching schematic: circuit shape, marker motion between checkpoints, speed/brake/throttle curves.
  • Not claimed: exact car GPS position, continuous gaps for all laps, causal tyre degradation, or live race telemetry.
READY-TO-RUN / 5–7 MINUTE TASK

Can a novice reconstruct Hamilton vs Leclerc?

“You missed the 2025 Las Vegas Grand Prix. Using this interface only: on lap 2, who was ahead—Lewis Hamilton or Charles Leclerc? Which of them made their first pit stop earlier, and on what laps? Then check qualifying: did Hamilton reach Q2, and who set the quicker Q1 lap?”

Observer checklist

Ask the participant to think aloud. Note hesitation, requests for help, misread labels, and tooltip use. Do not coach until the task is finished.

Expected answers · observer only

At lap 2: Leclerc P8, Hamilton P12. Leclerc stopped on lap 24, Hamilton on lap 29. Hamilton was eliminated in Q1 (1:57.115); Leclerc's Q1 was 1:54.814, 2.301 seconds quicker.

What success would mean

The participant finds the information without external searching or being told which control to click, uses a tooltip if a term is unfamiliar, and can state the comparison correctly. Record completion time and wrong turns, not just task success.

Prepared as an interactive research prototype, not a released F1 timing product.
